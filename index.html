How ShifaaLink could work

From confusion to coordinated care.

A responsible service depends on verified provider data, clear pricing, privacy, and partnerships—not guesswork.

STEP 01

Verified information

Hospitals and pharmacies update service availability, queue estimates, and prices with timestamps.

STEP 02

Compare options

Patients compare distance, estimated wait, service fees, and accessibility in one place.

STEP 03

Plan the visit

Appointment requests and queue notifications help spread demand where providers support them.

STEP 04

Protect patients

Show sources and update times, minimize personal data, and route medical decisions to professionals.
